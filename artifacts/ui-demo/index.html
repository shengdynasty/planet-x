    Planet X UI demo

    A UI prototype for the Space Apps challenge “Planet X and SPHEREx”. Everything shown is invented placeholder data: the sky texture, the scans, the candidates and their numbers. Nothing here is a detection.

    How the real version works

    • Pick a patch of sky; the app finds every SPHEREx exposure that covers it (IRSA Simple Image Access).
    • Small cutouts of each epoch are aligned and compared. Sources that shift against the fixed stars are flagged.
    • Flags are cross‑matched against JPL’s known asteroids and comets. Anything left over is a candidate, never a detection.
