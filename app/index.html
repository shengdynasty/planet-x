Planet X

A tool for the Space Apps challenge “Planet X and SPHEREx”: look for a distant planet as a very slow mover between SPHEREx’s six‑monthly all‑sky scans. Anything that moves and doesn’t match a known object is a candidate for a human to review, not a confirmed object.

Views

  • Sky: the whole sky with every flagged mover. Pick a marker, step through scans on the timeline, or Blink between them.
  • Compare: two scans of the selected mover with a wipe, a difference image, or blink.
  • Field: analyse one patch of sky: align the epochs, subtract, find what moved, and cross‑match it.
  • Movers panel: review candidates. Filter, sort, mark them follow‑up or rejected, and add notes.

Moving Sky demo

Open SPHEREx Moving Sky: the detection pipeline step by step on one simulated field, with blink, difference and track views and a vetting panel for each flag.

Simulated or live

The badge in the top bar says where the data comes from. Simulated data means placeholder scans and movers made up for the demo; Live · SPHEREx means real SPHEREx images fetched from IRSA through the local server, cross‑matched against JPL’s small‑body catalogue.
