The problem

A distant planet looks like a star that barely moves

Faint

Far from the Sun it reflects little light and glows mostly in the infrared.

Slow

At hundreds of AU it shifts only a little between images taken months apart.

Crowded

Asteroids, comets, variable stars and detector glitches all look like change too.

The only way to find it is to compare the same sky at different times and ask: what moved, and is it something we already know?
